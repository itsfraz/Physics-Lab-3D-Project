What is Kirchhoff's Voltage Law?

KVL (Loop Rule) is a consequence of the Law of Conservation of Energy. Because the electric electrostatic field is conservative, any charge that travels around a complete closed loop returns to its exact starting electric potential.

Mathematical Statement
Σ Vloop = 0
Total Potential Rises = Total Potential Drops
🏔️ The Mountain Analogy

Think of a circuit as a hiking loop: The battery is a ski lift that takes you up +12 meters. The three resistors are three downward hiking paths dropping you by -5m, -4m, and -3m. When you return to the base lodge, your net altitude change is exactly 0 meters!

Standard Loop Sign Rules

When traversing a closed loop in a chosen direction (CW or CCW):

Across Battery / Source:
Negative → Positive (− to +): +V (Rise)
Positive → Negative (+ to −): −V (Drop)
Across Resistors (Current I):
Along current direction: −I•R (Drop)
Against current direction: +I•R (Rise)
