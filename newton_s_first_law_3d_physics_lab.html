Real-World Physics

🧠 Think About This — Inertia in Everyday Life

Interact with both real-world scenarios below to see Newton's First Law in action.

Example 1 • Object at Rest

A Book on a Laboratory Table

State: Book at Rest (v = 0 m/s)

Why doesn't the book move on its own? The book stays still until an external force, such as your hand pushing it, acts on it. Once pushed, table friction acts as an opposing external force that slows it back to rest.

Example 2 • Object in Motion

Passenger in a Stopping Bus

Bus & Passenger moving together at 12 m/s

Why do you lurch forward? When a moving bus suddenly stops, the brakes apply force to the bus—not directly to your upper body. Due to inertia, your body tends to keep moving forward at the bus's original speed!
